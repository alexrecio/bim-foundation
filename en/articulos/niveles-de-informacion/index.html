      The problem

      What is an LOD 300?.

      The contract asks for an “LOD 300 model”. Whoever asks has one thing in mind; whoever models, another. Nobody knows what is delivered until it arrives.

      What the client asks for Door with data
      What the modeller delivers Heavily drawn door
      Publications that apply LOD 48% to the whole model, not to each element
      The specification says it plainly “There is no such thing as an LOD ### model”
      The principle

      As much as needed.

      And as little as possible. The information needed is not set by a number, but by four prior questions.

      01 · What forPurposecost, fire, coordination…
      02 · WhenMilestoneconcept design, construction design, handover…
      03 · WhoActorswho asks and who delivers
      04 · Of whatObjectdoor, wall, column, space…
      What is requested

      Three kinds of information.

      What is drawn, what is written and what is attached. Each one is requested separately and at its own level.

      Geometrical information

      Shape, in five questions.

      “More detail” says nothing. You specify how much detail, how many dimensions, how exact the position, what appearance and whether it can be modified.

      Detail From simple to fine
      Dimensionality 0D to 3D
      Location Relative or absolute
      Appearance Symbolic or realistic
      Parametric behaviour Fixed or editable
      Alphanumerical information

      The underlying confusion

      Drawn is not decided.

      A door can be drawn down to its handle and still not be decided. Detail is what goes in; development is what you can rely on.

      The hidden cost

      More is not better.

      Every piece of data requested has to be created, checked and maintained. What nobody will use is wasted work and noise for whoever is searching.

      Twenty years of acronyms

      From LOD to LOIN.

      It began as a software vendor's scale and has ended up as an ISO standard. Along the way it changed its name three times.

      Timeline
      ISO 7817-1:2024

      The standard in one diagram.

      Four prior questions and three kinds of information, each with its own aspects. It does not define numbered levels: it defines how to describe them.

      COBie

      What the maintainer needs.

      Quality control

      Four questions per delivery.

      Is it there, does it have a value, is the value valid and does the shape match what was asked for? The first three can be automated; the fourth, not entirely.

      01 · Is it there?The property existsin the right property set
      02 · Has it a value?It is not emptynor set to “pending”
      03 · Is it valid?Format and listEI2 60, not “60 min”
      04 · And the shape?Geometry reviewrules and an expert eye
      IDS
